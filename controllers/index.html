About FixIt

Built to give citizens a real voice

FixIt is a citizen-first platform developed to bridge the gap between communities and local governance. We believe that public spaces belong to everyone — and everyone has a stake in maintaining them.

Our system ensures no complaint is lost in bureaucracy. Every report is timestamped, tracked, and escalated automatically if not resolved within the defined SLA.
